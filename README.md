# 🦈 M5 Shark – Open-Source Flipper Zero Alternative

**M5 Shark** is a powerful, open-source IoT and RF hacking tool based on **M5StickC Plus** and **CC1101**. Designed as a real alternative to Flipper Zero, M5 Shark supports RF signal analysis, IR control, BadUSB payloads, and more.

> 💡 Perfect for ethical hackers, pentesters, makers, and developers who want full control over their gear.

---

## 🔥 Features

- ✅ **CC1101 RF Module** – Capture & replay RF signals (300–928 MHz)
- ✅ **IR Blaster** – Control TVs, devices, and send prank signals
- ✅ **BadUSB** – Run HID payloads for automation and pentesting
- ✅ **Custom Firmware** – 100% open source and expandable
- ✅ **OLED Display** – Real-time interaction and feedback
- ✅ **USB-C Powered** – Fast, modern, portable
